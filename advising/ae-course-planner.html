How to change your major to Aerospace Engineering
  1. If you are changing majors to a program in the College of Engineering but have not fully met the progression requirements for the upper division in the major, use the Major Declaration Form (PDF).
  2. If you have fully met the progression requirements for the upper division, use the Upper-Level Degree Progression (ULDP) Form (PDF). It lets you take the restricted upper-level classes, which are the ones "locked" on the flowchart.
  3. Review the B.S. in Aerospace Engineering flowchart (PDF) to see the course sequence and which classes are locked.
How to apply for the minor
  1. Download and complete the Aerospace Engineering minor application (PDF).
  2. Email the completed form to both Dr. Ajit Mujumdar at ajit@usf.edu and Ms. Kaylee Dunn at dunnk31@usf.edu.
Please review this plan with an advisor before you register. This planner is a guide to help you prepare for advising, not an official degree plan. Schedule a meeting with Ms. Maile Sinclair (msinclair@usf.edu) or Dr. Ajit Mujumdar (ajit@usf.edu) to confirm that the plan meets your degree requirements and fits your academic record.
Always check the live class schedule before you register. Course offerings change from year to year based on enrollment requests, so a course usually offered in the fall may be offered in the spring, or the other way around. The core and elective course lists and the flowchart may not reflect the latest schedule. Before you register, confirm each course for your term in the official USF class schedule.
